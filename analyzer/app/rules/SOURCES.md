## Deliberate limits

* A photo cannot measure the physical package volume (Rule 7(1), 12(7) for ≤10 cm³ packs), read a QR
  code's contents, tell a sticker from print with certainty (Rule 6(3)), or prove the corporate name is
  the registered one (Rule 10(2)). These surface as REVIEW for the inspector, never as PASS.
* The display-panel area for Rule 7 is estimated from the ArUco scale unless the inspector enters the
  panel size; borderline font heights go to REVIEW.
* E-commerce listing duties (Rule 6(10), 6(10A)) are platform obligations and are not checked on the pack.
* The FSS references cite the regulation; exact regulation sub-numbers should be confirmed against the
  current consolidated FSSAI text before prosecution use.
